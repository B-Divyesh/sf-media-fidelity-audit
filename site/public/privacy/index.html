Privacy

Media Fidelity Audit processes media only on your computer. The CLI does not upload files, collect analytics, or create an account.

The optional site license restore stores a license token and its verification timestamp in this browser’s local storage. A license is sent only to Sociobot’s verification endpoint when you restore or validate it. Purchases are handled by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record.

Contact the merchant of record through the receipt support channel for purchase privacy requests.
